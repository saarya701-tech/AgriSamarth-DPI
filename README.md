# 🌾 AgriSamarth — Digital Agriculture DPI Platform

🌐 **Live Website Application:** [https://agrisamarth-dpi.onrender.com]

---

## 📌 Overview
**AgriSamarth** is a climate-resilient, interoperable Digital Agriculture Public Good designed to empower Indian farmers with data-driven agronomic intelligence. Aligned with the national **AgriStack** framework, the platform integrates satellite GIS telemetry, real-time soil pH and temperature mapping, and crop health indices (NDVI) to deliver personalized, localized advisories. 

Powered by Google’s **Gemini 2.5** models, AgriSamarth offers instant computer vision leaf pathology diagnostics with organic remediation steps and features **Kisan Mitra**, an empathetic, multilingual AI chatbot providing field-specific guidance in Hindi, Gujarati, Marathi, and English.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* 🆔 **AgriStack Registry & Identity Sync:** Seamless integration with farmer identity, land holding records, and crop history.
* 🛰️ **Satellite GIS & Environmental Telemetry:** Auto-detects real-time ambient temperature, soil pH, and NDVI crop health indices via interactive plot mapping.
* 🌿 **AI Leaf Pathology Diagnostics:** Uses Gemini 2.5 Flash Vision to inspect crop foliage, filter non-plant images, and provide organic disease remedies and preventive steps.
* 🤖 **"Kisan Mitra" Multilingual AI Chatbot:** Context-aware conversational AI assistant tailored to individual farmer soil and field conditions (Supports Hindi, Gujarati, Marathi & English).
* 📄 **Digital Advisory & Printable Slips:** Synthesizes precision irrigation, bio-fertilizer blends, and quality control protocols with 1-click WhatsApp sharing, voice narration, and printable PDF slips.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Backend:** Python (FastAPI, Uvicorn, Pydantic, Pillow)
* **AI Models:** Google Gemini 2.5 Flash (Vision & Multilingual Conversational AI)
* **Frontend:** HTML5, Tailwind CSS, JavaScript
* **Mapping & GIS:** Leaflet.js, OpenStreetMap, SoilGrids API, Open-Meteo API
